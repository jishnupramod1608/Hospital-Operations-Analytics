
# Hospital Operations & Patient Analytics
## Project Insights and Business Recommendations

### 1. Project Overview
The objective of this project is to analyze hospital emergency room data to understand patient admissions, demographics, waiting times, and satisfaction.

The analysis uses 9,216 patient visit records.

### 2. Key Findings

#### Patient Visits
- Total patient visits: 9,216.
- Patient visits are distributed across different age groups, genders, and racial categories.
- The dashboard provides monthly trends to observe changes in patient volume.

#### Patient Admissions
- Admission rate is approximately 50%.
- The dashboard compares admitted and non-admitted visits.
- Admission patterns can be explored across different age groups.

#### Patient Demographics
- Gender distribution is approximately balanced.
- The 18–34 age group has the highest number of visits in the displayed analysis.
- Race-wise patient distribution helps understand the composition of hospital visits.

#### Waiting-Time Analysis
- Overall average waiting time is approximately 35.3 minutes.
- Waiting times vary across departments.
- The heatmap highlights departments and months with relatively higher waiting times.

#### Patient Satisfaction
- Average patient satisfaction is approximately 4.99 out of 5.
- Satisfaction scores provide an additional perspective on the patient experience.

### 3. Business Recommendations

1. **Improve Waiting-Time Management:** Review departments and months with higher average waiting times to identify operational bottlenecks.
2. **Optimize Staffing:** Use monthly patient visit trends to support workforce and resource planning.
3. **Monitor Admission Patterns:** Analyze admission rates across age groups to understand differences in patient requirements.
4. **Improve Patient Experience:** Continue monitoring satisfaction scores and investigate areas where patient feedback indicates opportunities for improvement.
5. **Use Regular Performance Reviews:** Monitor key indicators through periodic dashboard reviews.

### 4. Conclusion
This project demonstrates how SQL and Power BI can be combined to transform hospital emergency room data into meaningful operational insights.

The dashboard supports data-driven analysis of patient admissions, demographics, waiting times, and satisfaction.
